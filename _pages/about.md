Ranjana Thachangade is a doctoral student in polymer science currently based at Nicolaus Copernicus University in Torun, Poland.

Her research focuses on the preparation and characterization of polymers and polymer complexes for biomedical, environmental, and energy-related applications. She has worked across biomaterials, chitosan-based systems, silicone elastomers, and advanced polymer processing, with experience spanning synthesis, characterization, and additive manufacturing.

Her recent work includes research publications, patent applications, and conference presentations in polymer science and biomaterials. A full academic CV is available on the [CV](/cv/) page.
